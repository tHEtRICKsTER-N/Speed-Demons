How to play

⌨️ Keyboard

  • ←→ / AD steer · ↑/W gas · ↓/S brake
  • Space nitro · R back to checkpoint · Esc pause · F full screen
  • In the air: ↑/↓ flip, ←/→ spin, Q/E barrel roll

📱 Phone

  • The car accelerates by itself. Hold ◀ ▶ to steer.
  • ⚡ NITRO for speed, BRAKE for tight corners.
  • In the air those two buttons flip you forwards and backwards, and ◀ ▶ spin.
  • 🏆 Land flips, spins and rolls for stunt points and nitro. Chain them for a combo!
  • Grab coins, and earn more from stunts and your finishing place. Spend them in the Garage on new cars and upgrades.
  • 💨 Tuck in right behind a rival to slipstream, and pass them close for a close-pass bonus. Both fill your nitro.
  • 🎁 Come back every day for a bigger daily reward.
  • ⭐ Each track has 3 stars: finish, win, and beat the stunt target.
